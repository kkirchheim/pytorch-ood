OpenMIBOOD
==========

Datasets used by the medical imaging benchmarks from
*OpenMIBOOD: Open Medical Imaging Benchmarks for Out-Of-Distribution Detection* (CVPR 2025).
See :class:`pytorch_ood.benchmark.MIDOG_OpenMIBOOD`, :class:`pytorch_ood.benchmark.PhaKIR_OpenMIBOOD`
and :class:`pytorch_ood.benchmark.OASIS3_OpenMIBOOD`.
